# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages in `artifacts/shopify-optimizer`
- Public legal pages in `artifacts/shopify-optimizer`
- Public content/discovery pages such as landing, about, FAQ, blog, changelog, contact, affiliate, and case-study routes
- Shared crawlability infrastructure affecting public pages, including `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `llms.txt`, canonical handling, and the static HTML shell

## Out of scope
- Authenticated admin routes (`/home`, `/admin/**`, `/projects/**`)
- Client dashboard routes (`/client/**`)
- Invite, login, password reset, and other account-management flows unless they affect public crawlability
- API endpoints that are not part of public crawlability or metadata delivery

## Target audience
- Spanish-speaking Shopify merchants and e-commerce brands evaluating an AI optimization agency/platform
