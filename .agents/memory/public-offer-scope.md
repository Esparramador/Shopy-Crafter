The public offer must distinguish delivered client work from products still in development. The user explicitly wants Comic Crafter identified as being in development and creation.

**Why:** The user reported that the public presentation did not reflect the requested project status.

**How to apply:** Check project cards and related descriptions for consistency rather than treating a development project as a completed customer success.

The user wants artistic QR work presented without the “QR en 3D” offering. This does not mean removing the separate animated 3D gift/video service.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to leave only “QR artísticos” while also requesting animated 3D gifts in the shop.

**How to apply:** Keep these two offerings separate when revising the portfolio and shop; clarify before deleting QR stand products.

The user requests normal page scrolling, four packs including a custom option, and a shop covering physical laser products and digital creative services.

**Why:** These were explicit requirements repeated in the user's review of the earlier work.

**How to apply:** Plan improvements around the existing product rather than introducing scroll-dependent navigation or treating the shop as a separate unrelated product.
