# CartCraft

Friendly, trustworthy, cart-obsessed.

## Overview

CartCraft is a design system for general-purpose online retail stores that prioritizes trustworthiness and a seamless shopping experience. The balanced standard density ensures products are easy to compare without overwhelming the page. A green primary conveys reliability and go-ahead confidence at checkout, blue secondary reinforces trust, and orange tertiary highlights promotions. Every component is optimized for clarity, making the path from product discovery to completed order feel effortless.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** display trust signals (secure checkout badge, return policy, ratings) prominently near the CTA.
- **Do** keep the checkout flow to 3 steps or fewer; show a progress indicator at each step.
- **Do** use the orange tertiary sparingly for sale prices alongside a strikethrough original in Text Tertiary.
- **Do** enable side-by-side product comparison with a sticky comparison bar at the bottom.
- **Do** show estimated delivery dates on every product page, not just at checkout.
- **Don't** hide the total price until the final checkout step; display running totals in the cart drawer.
- **Don't** use the primary green for decorative elements; reserve it for actionable, positive-intent targets.
- **Don't** require account creation before checkout; always offer a guest checkout path.
- **Don't** auto-remove items from the cart after a timeout; respect the user's intent.
- **Don't** place promotional banners above the primary navigation; keep the nav always accessible.
