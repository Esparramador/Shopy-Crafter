# QuickMart

Efficient, no-frills, speed-to-checkout.

## Overview

QuickMart is a high-density design system engineered for grocery delivery and convenience store apps. Every decision optimizes speed to cart: compact spacing, bold price typography, and a familiar tri-color palette that communicates actions instantly. The dense layout maximizes visible products per screen while maintaining scannability. It is functional, trustworthy, and never in the way.

## Colors

- **Primary** (#DC2626): Red — CTAs, promotions, price highlights
- **Secondary** (#16A34A): Green — confirmations, in-stock, add-to-cart
- **Tertiary** (#2563EB): Blue — links, info actions, delivery badges
- **Neutral** (#6B7280): Gray — supporting UI, icons, dividers

- **Surface** (#F9FAFB): Cards, panels, category trays
- **Success** (#16A34A)

## Do's and Don'ts

1. **Do** make prices the most prominent element on product cards — use Body Large weight 700 in #DC2626 for sale prices.
2. **Don't** hide the "Add to Cart" button behind hover states — it must always be visible and tappable.
3. **Do** show delivery time estimates prominently using Blue #2563EB badges near the top of the viewport.
4. **Don't** use animations longer than 150ms — speed is the brand promise.
5. **Do** display unit prices ($/kg, $/lb) in Body Small directly beneath the main price.
6. **Don't** use more than 4 grid columns on mobile — legibility of small product tiles degrades quickly.
7. **Do** keep the sticky cart bar always visible at the bottom with item count, total, and checkout CTA.
8. **Don't** use the Green secondary for anything except positive confirmations and "Add to Cart" — mixing signals slows decisions.
9. **Do** support quantity steppers (+/-) directly on product cards to reduce taps to purchase.
10. **Do** make category navigation horizontally scrollable with chip-style filters for fast aisle switching.
