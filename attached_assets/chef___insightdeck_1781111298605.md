# InsightDeck

Polished, presentation-ready, persuasive.

## Overview

InsightDeck is a design system built for business intelligence and reporting dashboards where data clarity and visual storytelling take center stage. A teal-driven palette conveys analytical trust, while purple and pink accents draw attention to key insights.

## Colors

- **Primary** (#0D9488): Core actions, chart highlights, active navigation states
- **Secondary** (#9333EA): Data series accents, premium features, insight callouts
- **Tertiary** (#EC4899): Trend indicators, alert badges, secondary data highlights
- **Background** (#F8FAFC): Global page background
- **Surface** (#FFFFFF): Cards, panels, chart containers
- **Success** (#10B981)
- **Warning** (#F59E0B)
- **Error** (#EF4444)
- **Info** (#3B82F6)

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do give every chart a clear title and subtitle explaining the insight
- Don't use more than 5 colors in a single chart
- Do provide a "presentation mode" that strips chrome and maximizes chart area
