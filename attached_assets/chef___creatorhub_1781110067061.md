# CreatorHub Design System

## Overview

CreatorHub is an empowering, monetization-aware design system for creator economy and content monetization platforms. It balances creative expression with business clarity, using a coral-gold-teal palette that feels both energetic and trustworthy.

## Colors

- **Primary** (#F43F5E): Coral — primary CTAs, live indicators
- **Secondary** (#CA8A04): Gold — earnings, premium tiers
- **Tertiary** (#0D9488): Teal — growth metrics, community
- **Background** (#FFFFFF): Page background
- **Surface** (#F9FAFB): Cards, panels
- **Success** (#16A34A)
- **Warning** (#D97706)
- **Error** (#DC2626)
- **Info** (#2563EB)

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do use the gold color exclusively for earnings and monetary information
- Don't mix gold and coral in the same component
- Do use teal for positive growth metrics and community features
