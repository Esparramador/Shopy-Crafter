# Gray Paper

## Overview
Gray Paper is a dignified, editorial design system inspired by the tradition of serious journalism and long-form storytelling. Serif-dominant typography, restrained colors, and generous whitespace create an atmosphere of intellectual authority and reading comfort. The system treats every article as a crafted experience, prioritizing sustained attention over quick consumption.

## Colors
- **Primary** (#567B95): Byline links, section navigation accents, interactive highlights — Slate Blue
- **Primary Hover** (#456A84): Hover/pressed state for primary actions — Deep Slate
- **Secondary** (#326891): Featured article accents, opinion section highlights — Steel Blue
- **Neutral** (#F7F7F5): Page backgrounds, off-white reading surface — Warm Ivory
- **Background** (#FFFFFF): Article body background, clean reading canvas — White
- **Surface** (#F0F0ED): Card backgrounds, sidebar panels, pullquote backgrounds — Light Parchment
- **Text Primary** (#121212): Headlines, body text, all primary content — Near Black
- **Text Secondary** (#5A5A5A): Bylines, captions, secondary metadata — Medium Gray
- **Border** (#DFDFDF): Section dividers, article separators, horizontal rules
- **Success** (#2E7D32): Positive indicators, verified badges — Dark Green
- **Warning** (#E65100): Corrections, editor's notes — Burnt Orange
- **Error** (#C62828): Retractions, breaking alerts — Deep Red

## Typography
- **Display Font**: Playfair Display — loaded from Google Fonts
- **Body Font**: Libre Baskerville — loaded from Google Fonts
- **Code Font**: Source Code Pro — loaded from Google Fonts

Playfair Display anchors the editorial identity with high-contrast serifs perfect for headlines. It is used at 700 and 900 weights with default letter-spacing — the natural elegance of the letterforms requires no tracking adjustments. Libre Baskerville serves all body text at 400 weight with 1.8 line-height, optimized for sustained reading at 18px on screen. Italic style (400i) is used for pullquotes, book titles, and editorial emphasis. Bylines and metadata use Libre Baskerville at 400 weight, 12px, with 0.03em tracking in uppercase. Source Code Pro appears in data tables, interactive features, and technical articles at 14px/400.

Type scale: 12px (metadata/overline), 14px (caption/sidebar), 16px (body small), 18px (body/article text), 22px (h5/subheadline), 28px (h4/section headline), 36px (h3/article headline), 48px (h2/feature headline), 64px (h1/Sunday front page).

## Elevation
Gray Paper avoids shadows entirely in its editorial layouts, relying on whitespace, borders, and typographic hierarchy to create structure. The only shadow in the system is `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` applied to sticky navigation on scroll and modal overlays. Section separation uses 1px #DFDFDF horizontal rules or 3px #121212 bold section dividers (a classic newspaper convention). Pullquotes are distinguished by a left 3px #567B95 border and #F0F0ED background, not by shadow. This flat approach preserves the clean, printed-page aesthetic.

## Spacing
- Base unit: 4px
- Scale: 4px, 8px, 12px, 16px, 24px, 32px, 40px, 48px, 64px, 80px, 120px
- Component padding: Buttons 12px 20px, article text 0px (relies on max-width), inputs 12px 16px
- Section spacing: 48px between major sections, 24px between articles
- Container max width: 680px for article body text (optimal reading measure), 1200px for section pages, 1440px for homepage grid
- Card grid gap: 24px between article cards, 48px between sections on the homepage

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do use Playfair Display exclusively for headlines and display text — never for body copy
- Do maintain the 680px max-width for article body text to preserve optimal reading measure (65-75 characters per line)
- Don't use border-radius on any structural element; the sharp-cornered aesthetic is essential to the editorial identity
- Do use generous line-height (1.8) for body text to support long-form, immersive reading
- Don't use saturated colors for decorative purposes; the palette is deliberately muted and restrained
- Do employ bold horizontal rules (3px #121212) to separate major sections, mimicking newspaper layout
- Don't use icons where text labels suffice — the design language favors words over symbols
- Do use italic Libre Baskerville for pullquotes, emphasis, and editorial voice
- Don't add animation or motion effects; the system should feel static and contemplative, like a printed page
- Do treat whitespace as a primary design element — it signals quality and intentionality
