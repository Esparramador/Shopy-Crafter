Necesitamos usar la técnica de "Chain Prompting" (prompts encadenados). 
Cada bloque de 2 o 4 segundos debe retomar el estado final del anterior.Aquí tienes la secuencia maestra profesional para un producto (usaremos un Smartphone de Titanio como ejemplo, pero puedes sustituir el sujeto) dividida por tiempos para lograr la deconstrucción y construcción total:

FASE 1: DECONSTRUCCIÓN (0-10 Segundos)
00-02s (Inicio de fragmentación):Extreme macro of a sleek titanium smartphone. The screen subtly vibrates and begins to lift. Micro-bolts unscrew in mid-air. Soft cinematic studio lighting, shallow depth of field, 8k, photorealistic.
02-04s (Expansión de componentes):The smartphone internal components expand outwards. Camera lenses, circuit boards, and battery floating in a perfect 'exploded view' arrangement. High-speed camera tracking, metallic reflections, particles in the air.
04-06s (Desintegración atómica):The components break down further into raw materials. Glass melting into droplets, metal turning into liquid mercury-like spheres. Weightless environment, dark void background with volumetric rim lighting.
06-08s (Caos organizado - Timelapse):Timelapse of floating raw materials swirling in a vortex. Swarms of nanobots moving between liquid metal and glass shards. Motion blur, high energy, neon blue data streams connecting the parts.
08-10s (Estado de suspensión máxima):Full screen of floating microscopic tech-dust and liquid gold filaments. The camera moves through the debris. The 'deconstruction' is complete. High contrast, Octane Render style.

FASE 2: CONSTRUCCIÓN Y ENSAMBLAJE (10-20 Segundos)
10-12s (Reversión y núcleo):The vortex stops. A central glowing core appears. Liquid metal begins to wrap around an invisible frame. First stage of re-construction. Magnetic pull effect, sharp focus.
12-14s (Estructura interna):Internal architecture snaps together. Motherboard and CPU landing with precision. Sparks of electricity as connections are made. Macro view of golden pins aligning perfectly.
14-16s (Capas exteriores):The titanium frame slides over the internals from four angles. Camera lenses rotate and lock into place with a mechanical 'click' visual effect. Professional product lighting.
16-18s (Sellado de pantalla):The glass screen descends slowly, glowing with a soft light. As it touches the frame, a ripple effect clears the dust. The phone becomes a solid unit. Hyper-realistic reflections.
18-20s (Finalización y Power-on):The finished smartphone rotates to the front. The screen lights up with a vibrant 3D wallpaper. Final hero shot, elegant camera pull-back, luxury commercial aesthetic.

FASE 3: VARIACIONES PARA CONTINUAR HASTA 40S (Detalle Mega-Pro)
Para llegar a los 40 segundos, repetimos el ciclo con enfoques distintos (Materiales, Pruebas de Resistencia o Modelos):

20-24s (Virtual Try-On / Integración):The phone flies into the hand of a digital model. Perfect skin-to-metal contact. The model interacts with the screen, showing the UI. Natural daylight transition.

24-30s (Timelapse de entorno):The phone stays static while the background changes from a desert to a cyberpunk city in a fast timelapse. Lighting shifts dynamically over the titanium body.

30-40s (Macro de materiales y cierre):
Ultra-macro slow motion of the textures: the brushed metal, the lens coating, the glass edge. Ending with the brand logo laser-etched onto the back in a shower of sparks.
