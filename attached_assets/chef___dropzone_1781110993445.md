# DropZone

Bold, hype-driven, streetwear energy.

## Overview

DropZone is a design system for streetwear drops and limited-edition product launches where scarcity creates demand and bold visuals fuel the hype. Built on a dark foundation, the system uses high-contrast neon accents to punch through the darkness with red-orange fire, electric cyan, and signal yellow. Standard density maintains a high-impact visual hierarchy where every element fights for attention in controlled, deliberate ways. Sharp corners and colored glow shadows channel the raw energy of street culture.

## Colors

- **Primary** (#FF3D00): Primary CTAs, drop alerts, buy-now buttons
- **Secondary** (#00E5FF): Links, countdown timers, feature highlights
- **Tertiary** (#FFEA00): Sale badges, limited-edition markers, star ratings
- **Background** (#121212): Global page background, deep black base
- **Surface** (#1E1E1E): Product cards, panels, cart drawer
- **Success** (#00E676)
- **Warning** (#FFEA00)
- **Error** (#FF1744)
- **Info** (#00E5FF)

- **Code**: Space Mono 14px regular, 1.5 line height. Countdown digits, order IDs.

## Elevation

DropZone uses colored brand shadows that create a neon glow effect on the dark background.
- **Subtle:** 2px offset, 4px blur, #000000 at 40%
- **Medium:** 4px offset, 12px blur, #000000 at 50%, 20px glow #FF3D00 at 8%
- **Large:** 8px offset, 24px blur, #000000 at 60%, 40px glow #FF3D00 at 12%
- **Overlay:** 20px offset, 50px blur, #000000 at 80%
- **Glow Orange:** 20px glow #FF3D00 at 30%, 60px glow #FF3D00 at 10% — Hero CTAs, featured drop cards
- **Glow Cyan:** 20px glow #00E5FF at 25%, 60px glow #00E5FF at 8% — Countdown timers, secondary highlights

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** use countdown timers prominently for upcoming drops; the secondary cyan glow makes them impossible to miss.
- **Do** lean into bold, oversized Bebas Neue headlines with generous tracking for maximum impact.
- **Do** display stock quantities ("12 left") in real-time near the buy button to drive urgency.
- **Do** use the orange glow shadow on the primary CTA during active drops to create visual heat.
- **Do** design for mobile-first; most drop traffic comes from social media links on phones.
- **Don't** use rounded corners on product cards; the sharp 0px radius is core to the streetwear edge.
- **Don't** soften the dark background with grays lighter than #2A2A2A; maintain the high-contrast dramatic feel.
- **Don't** use the primary red-orange for body text or passive elements; it must always signal action or urgency.
- **Don't** add playful animations or bounce effects; transitions should be fast (150ms) and sharp, not cute.
- **Don't** fabricate scarcity or fake countdown timers; the hype must be authentic or trust collapses instantly.
